[h1]experienceWorks[/h1]

Lore, classified research, survivor knowledge, and experience-progression infrastructure for theWorks ecosystem.

experienceWorks provides persistent knowledge, research archives, progression-aware actions, and recoverable Survivor Journal data for Works modules and compatible projects.

[b]Mod Version: 0.12.2[/b]

[b]Development Status[/b]

The framework core is implemented and host-tested; native presentation and in-game multiplayer validation remain in development.

[h2]Current Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Canonical experienceWorks Mod ID, Lua API, registry, and persistent ledger
[*]Provider-owned Project Archive registry
[*]Namespaced knowledge definitions with provenance
[*]Composable ALL, ANY, and NOT prerequisites with legacy-list compatibility
[*]Semantic actions with weighted skills, bounded failure credit, diminishing returns, vanilla XP routing, and uncertain-award quarantine
[*]Feature-specific in-game validation commands with automatic human-readable result export
[/list]

homeWorks is the internal experienceWorks subsystem that owns knowledge, literature, research, study, and Survivor Journal systems. It is not a separate mod.

[h2]homeWorks Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Character-persistent, idempotent knowledge unlocks and completed-reading state
[*]Cross-references, rarity tiers, restricted spawn metadata, and read-completion requirements
[*]Canonical and genuinely redacted research presentation
[*]Provider-owned literature, research documents, and knowledge definitions
[*]Interruption-safe Survivor Journals with progressive physical documentation
[*]Persistent single-successor claims, duplicate-payload rejection, and exhausted keepsakes
[*]Non-additive recovery planning that never reduces a successor's existing progression
[/list]

[h2]Ecosystem Integration[/h2]
[list]
[*]Provides shared research and knowledge infrastructure for Works modules and Projects
[*]Supports provider-owned archives, documents, semantic actions, and knowledge definitions
[*]Provides Survivor Journal infrastructure for recoverable survivor progression
[*]Supports optional uiWorks knowledge presentation
[*]Has no required mod dependencies
[*]Migrates persisted data from the former misspelled identity without retaining it as public API
[/list]

[h2]Upcoming Features[/h2]
[list]
[*]Recipe and contextual-action knowledge consumers
[*]Native Survivor Journal timed actions and inventory presentation
[*]Verified raw-XP restoration and multiplayer validation
[/list]

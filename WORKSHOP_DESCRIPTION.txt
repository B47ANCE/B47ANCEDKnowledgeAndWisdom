[h1]B47ANCED Knowledge & Wisdom[/h1]

Lore, classified research, survivor knowledge, and experience-progression infrastructure for B47ANCE'd mods and compatible providers.

B47ANCED Knowledge & Wisdom provides persistent knowledge, research archives, progression-aware actions, and recoverable Survivor Journal data for B47ANCE'd mods and compatible projects.

[b]Mod Version: 0.16.0[/b]

[b]Development Status[/b]

Development build. Multiplayer gameplay is disabled pending private server persistence and identity/migration implementation. Native player ModData and GlobalModData are not private authority stores. Lua host checks pass; the renamed identity, old-save loading and native presentation still require in-game validation. Dedicated multiplayer NOT TESTED. G7 and stable-release G8 BLOCKED.

[h2]Authority and Configuration Repairs[/h2]

Read-only queries leave saved data untouched. Recovery plans validate persistent exhaustion and successor locks. Repeated acquisitions preserve original replay identity. Malformed saves are rejected without resetting them. Expired progression counters are pruned without evicting active or uncertain awards. Server sandbox controls cover XP and journal policies.

[h2]Identity Upgrade[/h2]

Use Mod ID B47ANCEDKnowledgeAndWisdom and update dependent Lua imports and mod lists together. Remove the old package before enabling the renamed identity. Private saved-data keys and replay records are retained; older saved item types remain recognized. Existing consumers need corresponding updates.
